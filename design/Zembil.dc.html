Store spines carry the only other hues (green, violet) and are decoration, never the sole carrier of meaning. Spacing scale: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 28. Radii: 11 rows-tick, 14 chips, 18 buttons, 22 cards, 28 sheets.

Ticking & re-sorting — motion notes
0 msTap anywhere on the row. Tick box scales to 0.86 and back (140 ms, spring), row background jumps to green tint immediately, haptic tap. The state is committed locally before any network call.
140 msRows below slide up and the ticked row travels down to the top of the basket group — FLIP transform, 260 ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Nothing fades out; the row is never destroyed and re-created, so the eye can follow it.
400 msRow settles with the 2px green ring and its 44px Undo button. Header count re-numbers with no animation.
6 sRing and Undo button decay to the quiet history style (200 ms crossfade). The tick itself stays full-size and tappable forever — un-ticking is the same gesture, reversed, and the row FLIPs back up.
OfflineNo spinner on rows. The change stays applied and the row gets a dashed border; the amber banner at the top of Shops owns the retry. If the server rejects it, the row shakes 6 px once and shows “Not saved — tap to retry”.
Someone elseAnother family member's tick arrives without motion — it appears already in the basket group, with a one-time 1 s soft highlight so you notice the list changed under you.
AddNew item flies in at the top of the to-buy group, 180 ms, and the sheet stays open with the field cleared so you can keep typing. Reduced-motion: all of the above collapse to a 100 ms opacity change.
